Privacy Policy

Last updated: [31-07-2026]

This Privacy Policy explains what information Bodyfuel ("we", "us", "the app") collects, how we use it, and your rights regarding your data.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account information: your email address, used to create and secure your account via Firebase Authentication.
  • Nutrition data: foods you log, water intake, weight entries, and your daily calorie goal. This is stored locally on your device (browser storage) and is not transmitted to our servers.
  • Subscription data: your premium status, subscription expiry date, and a payment reference ID (not your card details) if you subscribe to Premium.
  • AI Coach questions: if you use the AI Coach or Smart Suggestions feature, the text of your question and relevant nutrition context is sent to our AI provider (Groq) to generate a response. This is not stored permanently by us.

2. What We Do NOT Collect

  • We never see or store your payment card number, CVV, or expiry date. All payments are processed directly by Razorpay, our payment processor.
  • We do not collect your real name, phone number, or physical address.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide core app functionality (login, food logging, tracking).
  • To manage your Premium subscription status.
  • To respond to AI Coach questions you submit.

We do not sell your data to third parties, and we do not use your data for advertising.

4. Third-Party Services

We rely on the following third-party services to operate Bodyfuel:

  • Firebase (Google): authentication and database hosting.
  • Razorpay: payment processing for Premium subscriptions.
  • Groq: AI processing for the AI Coach and Smart Suggestions features.
  • Netlify: website hosting.

Each of these providers has its own privacy policy governing how they handle data on their end.

5. Data Storage & Security

Your account and subscription data is stored in Firebase Firestore, secured so that only your logged-in account can access your own data. Your day-to-day nutrition logs are stored locally on your own device.

6. Your Rights

You can request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by contacting us at [support.bodyfuel@gmail.com]. Since nutrition logs are stored on your device, clearing your browser data will also remove them.

7. Children's Privacy

Bodyfuel is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

9. Contact Us

Questions about this policy? Reach us at [support.bodyfuel@gmail.com].
